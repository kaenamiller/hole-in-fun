# A first round at Cedar House

1. Start at Cedar House in management mode. The starter has three holes, six facilities, three staff, and $180,000.
2. Let the clock run. Visitors arrive after about 12–24 in-game minutes. Use 4× speed to shorten the wait.
3. Open **Holes** and choose First Light. Run **Analyze this hole** to compare 30 rounds at each skill level. Clear the lines before resuming design.
4. Open **Terrain**. Choose a fairway or sand brush, move it onto the land, check the displayed price, and click. Undo with Command/Ctrl+Z. The grid and single-node toggle support precise height editing.
5. Create another hole with a tee click and a cup click. Paint a fairway route between them. Select the hole to rename it, set par, change its green radius, or add layup waypoints.
6. Use **Build** to add paths. Choose two dry endpoints; shared paths can carry carts. Water crossings require a bridge with two dry shores. If a route is impossible the game reports it and affected golfers leave safely.
7. Place scenery from one collection near other distinct items in that collection. Use the beauty overlay to inspect the area of influence.
8. Select a golfer, then use **Guests** to inspect their visit, needs, score and thought. **Follow selected guest** keeps the camera with them.
9. Use **Staff** to hire and assign workers. **Money** holds prices, daily reports, loans and repayments. **Events** shows physical prerequisites and a date selector for the calendar.
10. Save your resort from **Saves**, or press F5. Daily autosaves and pre-replacement saves offer additional recovery points.

All editing is live. Affected shots restart without a stroke penalty; compensation and refunds appear in the ledger. Closing the resort stops new arrivals. Pausing stops the simulation while allowing design.
