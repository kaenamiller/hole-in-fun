# A first round at Cedar House

1. Start at Cedar House in management mode. The starter has three holes, six facilities, three staff, and $180,000.
2. Let the clock run. Visitors arrive after about 12–24 in-game minutes. Use 4× speed to shorten the wait.
3. Open **Holes** and choose First Light. The hole opens in the inspector; run **Analyze** in the Shot lab to compare 30 rounds at each skill level. **Clear** removes the lines. The **‹ Holes** button at the top of the panel returns to the list.
4. Open **Terrain**. Choose a fairway or sand brush, move it onto the land, check the price in the tool badge, and click. Undo and redo live in the tool badge and on Command/Ctrl+Z. The **View** bar beside it holds the data overlays and the editing grid; the single-node toggle in the Brush card supports precise height editing.
5. Create another hole with a tee click and a cup click. Paint a fairway route between them. Select the hole to rename it, set par, change its green radius, or add layup waypoints.
6. Use **Build** to add paths. Choose two dry endpoints; shared paths can carry carts. Water crossings require a bridge with two dry shores. If a route is impossible the game reports it and affected golfers leave safely.
7. Place scenery from one collection near other distinct items in that collection. Use the beauty overlay to inspect the area of influence.
8. Click a golfer, staff member, building, or hole on the land to open it in the inspector. Guests show needs, score, and thought; **Follow** keeps the camera with them. Whatever is selected stays reachable from the pill at the top of any tab.
9. Use **Staff** to hire; click a team member to set shifts, assignments, and training. **Money** has Prices, Members, Loans, and Ledger views. **Reputation** holds rating, campaigns, and events with their prerequisites and a date selector. **Progress** shows grade requirements and research. **Reports** opens the wide charts and tables overlay.
10. Save your resort from the **⚙** menu in the header, or press F5. The **🔔** button opens the event log. Autosaves at each settlement and before replacing your current resort offer additional recovery points.

All editing is live. Affected shots restart without a stroke penalty; compensation and refunds appear in the ledger. Closing the resort stops new arrivals. Pausing stops the simulation while allowing design.

### Resort photography

Press **F9** to hide the interface, selection graphics and hole labels while retaining camera control. Press **F9** again or **Escape** to return. **F12** saves a screenshot in the game's user-data `screenshots` folder (on macOS, `~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Hole in Fun/screenshots/`).
